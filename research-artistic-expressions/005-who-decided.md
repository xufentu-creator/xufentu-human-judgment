## 中文

后来，

我越来越常听见一句话。

"不是我决定的。"

他说。

她说。

他们都这样说。

后来，

事情结束了。

结果留下了。

可是，

那个决定，

却没有主人。

有人说，

只是流程。

有人说，

只是规定。

有人说，

只是照着做。

于是，

每个人，

都只是经过。

没有人，

真正停下来。

有一天，

我开始想。

如果每一个人，

都不是决定的人。

为什么，

最后一定有人，

承担结果。

小时候，

我以为，

长大以后，

答案会越来越清楚。

后来才发现，

真正复杂的，

从来不是选择。

而是选择以后，

谁愿意站出来，

轻轻地说一句：

"这是我决定的。"

不是为了证明自己。

不是为了赢。

只是因为，

每一个结果，

都应该有人，

认真地面对。

后来，

我终于明白，

勇敢，

有时候不是向前走。

而是在所有人沉默的时候，

愿意说一句：

"让我来。"

---

### Chorus

是谁决定的？

风没有回答。

时间没有回答。

人群没有回答。

可是，

每一个结果，

都记得，

那个真正站出来的人。

是谁决定的？

答案，

也许不是名字。

而是，

有人愿意承担，

有人愿意留下。

## English

# Who Decided

As the years passed,

I kept hearing

the same sentence.

"It wasn't my decision."

He said it.

She said it.

They all did.

The outcome remained.

The decision remained.

Yet somehow,

the decision

had no owner.

Some blamed the process.

Some blamed the rules.

Some said,

"I was only following."

Everyone

passed through.

No one

stayed.

Then one day,

I wondered,

if no one decided,

why does someone

always live

with the result?

When I was young,

I believed

growing older

would make life clearer.

Instead,

I learned

that choosing

is rarely

the hardest part.

The hardest part

is quietly saying,

"Yes.

That was my decision."

Not to win.

Not to be praised.

Simply because

every outcome

deserves

someone

who will remain.

Perhaps courage

is not always

moving forward.

Sometimes,

it is being

the one person

who stays

after everyone else

has walked away.

---

### Chorus

Who decided?

The wind

never answered.

Time

never answered.

The crowd

never answered.

Yet every ending

remembered

the one

who stayed.

Who decided?

Perhaps

the answer

is never a name.

Perhaps

it is simply

the person

willing to remain.
