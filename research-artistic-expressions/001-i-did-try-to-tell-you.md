# I Did Try to Tell You

## 我不是没有说过

**Series:** Judgment Remains  
**Track:** 001  
**Author:** Xufen Tu  
**Status:** Public Draft

---

我不是没有说过，
只是风比我的声音更早到达。

我不是没有解释，
只是每个人都带着自己的答案。

我一遍一遍重复，
直到连自己都开始怀疑，
是不是从来没有开口。

后来我才知道，
有些人不是没有听见，
他们只是已经决定，
不会听见。

于是我开始沉默，
不是因为放弃，
而是因为真正重要的话，
不需要每天证明。

时间慢慢过去，
那些当初被忽略的话，
开始出现在别人嘴里。

他们说，
早就知道。

他们说，
一直都是这样。

没有人记得，
那个最早开口的人。

可是没有关系。

我不是为了证明自己。

我只是希望，
有人能够少走一点弯路。

如果今天，
终于有一个人停下来，
认真听完一句话。

那所有沉默，
都没有白费。

---

### English

I was never silent.

The wind simply arrived
before my voice did.

I was never unwilling to explain.

People had already brought
their own answers.

I repeated myself
until even I began to wonder
whether I had spoken at all.

Later,
I understood something.

Some people did hear.

They had simply decided
not to listen.

So I became quieter.

Not because I gave up.

But because
the most important words
do not need to be defended every day.

Time passed.

The words once ignored
slowly returned
through other voices.

People said,

"We always knew."

People said,

"It has always been this way."

No one remembered
who spoke first.

That is alright.

I was never speaking
to be remembered.

I only hoped
someone might lose
a little less time.

If today,
even one person
chooses to truly listen,

then none of the silence
was wasted.
