## 中文

你离开的那一天，

天气没有什么不同。

街上的车，

还是一辆接着一辆。

路边那家店，

照常开门。

楼下的咖啡，

还是那个味道。

世界，

一点也没有停下来。

只有我知道，

有一些东西，

已经回不到昨天。

后来，

我才明白，

真正的离开，

从来没有电影里的音乐。

没有慢镜头。

没有最后一句对白。

很多时候，

一句"明天见。"

就是最后一句。

一个转身。

就是最后一次回头。

后来，

我们学会了，

把很多名字，

放进通讯录。

却再也没有拨通。

把很多照片，

留在手机里。

却很少再打开。

把很多回忆，

放进心里。

却不敢再提起。

有人说，

时间会治愈一切。

可是时间，

只是教会我们，

如何带着想念，

继续生活。

直到有一天，

我终于能够，

笑着说起你的名字。

不是因为忘记。

而是因为，

你曾经来过。

所以我的生命，

才会变成今天的样子。

如果人生一定会有离别，

我希望，

每一次相遇，

都不要因为来得太匆忙，

而忘了认真看看彼此。

因为有一天，

我们都会成为，

别人故事里的那个人。

而能够留下来的，

从来不是拥有多久。

而是，

有没有真正陪伴过。

## English

# The Day You Left

The day you left,

nothing seemed different.

Cars still passed by.

The café downstairs

opened as usual.

The streets

kept moving.

The world

did not stop.

Only I knew

that something

would never return

to yesterday.

Later,

I learned

that real goodbyes

rarely come

with music.

No slow motion.

No final speech.

Sometimes,

"See you tomorrow"

becomes

the last sentence.

Sometimes,

one ordinary wave

becomes

the last goodbye.

We keep names

inside our phones,

yet never call.

We keep photographs,

yet rarely look.

We keep memories,

carefully folded

inside ourselves.

People say,

time heals.

Perhaps.

But more often,

time teaches us

how to continue

while carrying

what we miss.

One day,

I found myself

smiling

when I spoke your name.

Not because

I had forgotten.

But because

you had become

part of who I am.

If every life

must contain

goodbyes,

then let every meeting

be honest enough

to deserve remembering.

Because one day,

all of us

will become

someone else's memory.

And what remains

is never

how long we stayed.

Only

how deeply

we were there.

---

### Chorus

The day you left,

the world stayed the same.

Only my tomorrow

became different.

The wind

still moved

through the trees.

But every time

it found me,

it carried

your memory

home.
