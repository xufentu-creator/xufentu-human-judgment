# Still Waiting

## 还在等

**Series:** Judgment Remains  
**Track:** 003  
**Author:** Xufen Tu  
**Status:** Draft

## 中文

小时候，

总觉得，

等待是一件很短的事情。

等放学。

等长大。

等下一班车。

等一个电话。

等一句答案。

后来才知道，

人生很多时候，

都在等待。

等别人理解。

等自己勇敢。

等一句迟来的道歉。

等那个一直没有出现的人。

有时候，

我们以为，

自己在等机会。

后来才发现，

真正等待的，

其实是自己。

那些没有完成的梦想，

没有寄出的信，

没有拨出去的号码，

没有说出口的话，

一直静静地，

陪着我们长大。

有人说，

不要再等了。

向前走吧。

可是他们不知道，

有些等待，

不是停下脚步。

而是心里，

始终留着一个位置。

留给曾经相信的人。

留给曾经相信的自己。

很多年以后，

我终于明白，

等待，

从来不是浪费时间。

真正浪费的，

是忘记了为什么出发。

于是，

我继续生活。

继续工作。

继续爱身边的人。

继续认真走每一天。

只是心里，

依然保留着，

那个没有关上的窗。

因为我知道，

总有一天，

风会吹进来。

不是为了改变过去。

而是告诉今天的我，

所有认真等待过的人，

都没有白白走过这一段路。

---

### Chorus

我还在等。

不是等谁回来。

也不是等世界改变。

我只是相信，

每一份认真，

都会找到属于自己的时间。

我还在等。

不是因为停留。

而是因为希望，

从来没有离开。

## English

# Still Waiting

When I was young,

waiting

felt small.

Waiting for school to end.

Waiting to grow up.

Waiting for the next bus.

Waiting for a phone call.

Waiting for an answer.

Later,

I discovered

that life itself

is made of waiting.

Waiting to be understood.

Waiting to become brave.

Waiting for an apology

that never came.

Waiting for someone

who never arrived.

For years,

I thought

I was waiting

for opportunity.

Then I realized,

I was quietly waiting

to become

the person

I had always hoped to be.

The dreams

left unfinished.

The letters

never sent.

The numbers

never dialed.

The words

never spoken.

They all

grew older

with me.

People said,

"Stop waiting.

Move on."

They did not know

that some waiting

is not standing still.

It is leaving

a place in your heart

for what once mattered.

Years later,

I finally understood.

Waiting

is not wasted time.

Forgetting

why you began—

that is.

So I kept living.

Kept working.

Kept loving.

Kept walking.

And somewhere inside,

I left

one window open.

Because I believe

one day,

the wind

will come through.

Not to change yesterday,

but to remind me

that every sincere heart

eventually

finds its own season.

---

### Chorus

I am still waiting.

Not for someone to return.

Not for the world to change.

Only because

I still believe

that everything

done with sincerity

has its own time.

I am still waiting.

Not because I stopped.

But because

hope

never left.
