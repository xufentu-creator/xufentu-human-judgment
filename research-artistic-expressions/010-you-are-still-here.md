# You Are Still Here

## 你一直都在

**Track:** 010

**Status:** Draft

## 中文

很多年以后，

我开始明白，

有些人，

从来没有真正离开。

他们只是，

换了一种方式，

陪着我们。

有时候，

是一句话。

有时候，

是一首歌。

有时候，

是经过某个熟悉的地方，

忽然停下脚步。

风吹过的时候，

会想起一个笑容。

下雨的时候，

会想起一句叮嘱。

看见熟悉的颜色，

会想起某一天的黄昏。

原来，

记忆，

从来不是负担。

它只是，

生命留下来的温度。

小时候，

我害怕失去。

长大以后，

我才知道，

真正重要的人，

不会因为时间，

就从生命里消失。

他们说过的话，

会变成，

我们说出口的话。

他们给过的温柔，

会变成，

我们给予别人的温柔。

他们相信过我们，

于是，

我们开始相信自己。

后来，

我们也会遇见，

新的朋友。

新的家人。

新的孩子。

新的故事。

那些曾经得到过的善意，

会一点一点，

继续传下去。

就像春天，

不会停留在一棵树上。

而是，

慢慢长满整片山坡。

也许，

人生真正留下来的，

从来不是名字。

不是照片。

不是奖杯。

而是，

那些因为遇见一个人，

而悄悄改变的人。

如果有一天，

我也会离开。

我希望，

别人想起我的时候，

不是因为我做过什么。

而是因为，

曾经有一段时间，

我认真地陪他们走过。

这样，

就已经足够。

---

### Chorus

你一直都在。

不是站在昨天。

而是在今天，

每一个勇敢继续生活的人心里。

你一直都在。

因为爱，

从来不会停在告别。

它会继续，

变成新的光，

照亮后来的人。

## English

# You Are Still Here

Years have passed,

and I have learned

that some people

never truly leave.

They simply

find another way

to stay.

Sometimes,

it is a sentence

we still remember.

Sometimes,

a song.

Sometimes,

an ordinary street

that suddenly

feels familiar again.

The wind

carries a smile.

The rain

brings back a voice.

A single color

returns us

to another season.

Memory

is not a weight.

It is warmth

that refuses

to disappear.

When I was young,

I feared

losing people.

Now,

I understand

that those

who shaped our lives

continue living

through us.

Their kindness

becomes ours.

Their courage

finds another heart.

Their faith in us

teaches us

to believe

in ourselves.

Life moves on.

New friends arrive.

New families grow.

New stories begin.

The love

we once received

quietly becomes

the love

we give away.

Like spring,

it never belongs

to one tree.

It slowly

covers

an entire hillside.

Perhaps

what remains

after a lifetime

is never fame,

or photographs,

or achievements.

Perhaps

it is simply

the lives

that became gentler

because

we were here.

If one day

I also leave,

I hope

I am remembered

not for what I built,

but for

walking beside someone

when they needed

a companion.

For me,

that

would be enough.

---

### Chorus

You are still here.

Not inside yesterday,

but inside

every heart

that keeps choosing

to live with hope.

You are still here.

Because love

never ends

with goodbye.

It becomes

a quiet light,

guiding someone

we may never meet.
