# One More Time

## 如果还能再一次

## 中文

小时候，

总觉得，

机会很多。

今天错过了，

还有明天。

今年没做到，

还有明年。

后来，

时间越来越快。

有些人，

昨天还在身边。

今天，

就已经走远。

有些梦想，

一直放在那里。

想着，

等忙完这一阵。

等孩子长大。

等工作稳定。

等生活轻松一点。

可是后来，

生活一直没有变轻。

等待，

却慢慢变成了习惯。

直到有一天，

我发现，

原来最容易错过的，

不是机会。

而是，

那个愿意认真去做的自己。

如果还能再一次，

我不会等所有条件都刚刚好。

我会在害怕的时候开始。

会在不确定的时候迈出第一步。

因为后来才知道，

人生没有真正准备好的那一天。

真正开始的那一天，

就是最好的那一天。

---

### Chorus

如果还能再一次，

我不会等风停。

我会迎着风走。

因为很多路，

不是风停了，

才走得远。

而是走着走着，

风就变成了顺风。

## English

# One More Time

When I was young,

I believed

there would always

be another chance.

Tomorrow.

Next year.

Another season.

Life felt endless.

Then,

without asking,

the years

began to move faster.

People left.

Dreams waited.

Plans collected dust

on quiet shelves.

"I'll do it later."

"When life settles down."

"When the timing is right."

But life

never really slowed.

Only the waiting

grew older.

One day,

I realized

that the easiest thing

to lose

was not opportunity.

It was

the version of myself

who was still willing

to begin.

If I had

one more chance,

I wouldn't wait

for certainty.

I'd start

while afraid.

I'd walk

before the road

looked clear.

Because life

never announces

the perfect moment.

Sometimes,

the first step

creates it.

---

### Chorus

One more time.

I would not wait

for the wind

to disappear.

I'd walk

through it.

Because sometimes,

the wind

becomes your direction

only after

you begin.
