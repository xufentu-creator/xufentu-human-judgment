# If I Had Known

## 如果早一点知道

**Track:** 004

**Status:** Draft

## 中文

如果早一点知道，

有些路，

走过以后，

就再也回不去了。

那一天，

我会不会，

走得慢一点。

会不会，

再认真看一眼，

那个正在挥手的人。

人生很奇怪。

我们总是在后来，

才知道，

原来那一天，

就是最后一次。

最后一次，

一起回家。

最后一次，

一起吃饭。

最后一次，

说一句：

"明天见。"

后来，

明天来了。

那个人，

却再也没有出现。

于是，

我们开始学会，

把很多遗憾，

叫作成长。

把很多想念，

藏进沉默。

把很多眼泪，

留给夜晚。

可是时间，

从来没有回答。

如果重新来一次，

我会不会，

说得更多一点。

抱得更紧一点。

等得久一点。

勇敢一点。

没有人知道。

人生，

从来不会，

把答案提前告诉我们。

所以，

每一天，

都可能成为，

未来最想回去的一天。

于是，

我开始认真说：

谢谢。

开始认真说：

对不起。

开始认真说：

一路平安。

因为我终于明白，

很多话，

不是为了今天。

而是为了以后，

想起今天的时候，

不会留下遗憾。

---

### Chorus

如果早一点知道，

时间不会停留。

我一定会，

把今天，

过得更认真一点。

如果早一点知道，

离别没有预告。

我一定会，

把每一次相遇，

都当作礼物。

## English

# If I Had Known

If I had known,

some roads

could never

be walked twice,

perhaps

I would have slowed down.

Perhaps

I would have looked back

one more time.

Life is strange.

We usually discover

too late

that an ordinary day

was the last one.

The last walk home.

The last shared meal.

The last

"See you tomorrow."

Tomorrow came.

They did not.

So we learned

to rename regret

as growing up.

To hide longing

inside silence.

To leave tears

for the night.

Time,

however,

never answered.

If life began again,

would I speak

a little more?

Hold on

a little longer?

Wait

a little longer?

Be

a little braver?

No one knows.

Life never tells us

which moment

will become

our favorite memory.

So now,

I try

to say thank you

while people

can still hear it.

I try

to say I'm sorry

before silence arrives.

I try

to say goodbye

as if every goodbye

matters.

Because I finally learned,

some words

are not only

for today.

They are for

the person

we become

when we remember today.

---

### Chorus

If I had known

time never waits,

I would have lived

this day

more carefully.

If I had known

goodbyes

never send a warning,

I would have treated

every meeting

like a gift.
