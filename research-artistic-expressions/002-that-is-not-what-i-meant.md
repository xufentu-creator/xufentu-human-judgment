# That Is Not What I Meant

## 不是这个意思

**Series:** Judgment Remains  
**Track:** 002  
**Author:** Xufen Tu  
**Status:** Public Draft

---

我说：

不是这个意思。

可是已经没有人停下来。

他们开始解释我的解释。

替我补充。

替我定义。

替我完成一句
我还没有说完的话。

后来，

每个人都在回答。

却没有人再问。

他们相信自己的理解。

比相信我的表达，
更容易。

于是，

一句简单的话，

慢慢变成了另一个故事。

我开始删掉很多字。

因为越解释，

误会越长。

后来，

我终于明白。

真正理解一个人，

不是回答得最快。

而是愿意停下来，

把最后一句，

也听完。

如果有一天，

你真的听见我，

你会发现，

我从来没有复杂。

只是一直，

没有被真正理解。

### English

## That Is Not What I Meant

That wasn't

what I meant.

But no one stopped.

They explained

my explanation.

Finished

my unfinished sentence.

Added meanings

I never gave.

Soon,

everyone had an answer.

No one

had a question.

People trusted

their own understanding

more than

my own words.

One simple sentence

became

a completely different story.

So I spoke less.

Because sometimes,

the more we explain,

the larger

the misunderstanding grows.

Then I learned,

understanding someone

isn't answering first.

It is staying

long enough

to hear

the final sentence.

If one day

you truly hear me,

you may discover

I was never difficult.

I was simply

never fully understood.
