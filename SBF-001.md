# Structural Boundary Framework

**Author:** Xufen Tu
**Model ID:** SBF-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Domain:** Complex Systems · AI Governance · Decision Architecture
**Status:** Active

## Overview

The Structural Boundary Framework describes a simple observation.

In complex environments, decisions do not fail because of a lack of information.

They fail because the structure that connects information to action is unstable.

This framework focuses on that instability.

## Core Principle

Human judgment cannot be reduced to data or models.

It acts as a boundary — a point where information stops being processed and begins to be interpreted.

## Core Question

Where should human judgment remain present?

## Primary Function

SBF-001 identifies the boundary where information, automation, institutional pressure, and system momentum can no longer replace human judgment.

## Interface

Human judgment functions as a structural boundary in complex systems.
