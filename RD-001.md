# Responsibility Drift

**Author:** Xufen Tu
**Model ID:** RD-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Domain:** Complex Systems · AI Governance · Decision Architecture
**Status:** Active

## Overview

Responsibility Drift describes a structural pattern observed in complex systems.

As systems expand, decisions do not disappear.

Responsibility shifts across layers.

Over time, it becomes unclear who is actually making the decision.

## Core Observation

A decision may appear to be produced by a system.

In practice, it is distributed across multiple components.

Each part contributes.

No part fully owns the outcome.

## Role

This model identifies where responsibility becomes unclear.

It distinguishes between:

* a system producing outputs
* a human taking responsibility

A decision only exists when responsibility is assumed.

## Interface

A decision without ownership is not a decision.

## System Relation

Responsibility Drift explains why the boundary defined in Structural Boundary Framework (Tu, SBF-001) becomes unstable.

When responsibility is not clearly assigned, the structural boundary cannot hold.
