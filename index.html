Judgment Before Momentum

Research position

AI can accelerate information, generate options, and coordinate systems at scale. Speed does not remove uncertainty, consequence, context, or responsibility.

Human judgment remains structurally necessary where uncertainty, consequence, and responsibility cannot be fully transferred to automated outputs.

This public research examines when system momentum begins to exceed human judgment capacity, how responsibility becomes fragmented across people and systems, and why provenance, accountability, correction, and meaningful human oversight must remain visible before automated outputs become action.
