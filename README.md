# Xufen Tu · Public Research

**Xufen Tu** is an **Independent Interdisciplinary Researcher** working on questions of human judgment, responsibility, provenance, authorship, AI governance, and complex systems.

This repository is a public reading and citation collection. It contains selected research observations, public research positions, artistic expressions, authorship records, and links to formally published papers. The materials ask what happens to human responsibility when information and automated outputs circulate faster and more widely.

## Public boundary

This collection does not include private research records, internal methods, implementation materials, client information, or unpublished development work. Public observations express research questions and positions; they do not prescribe a universal decision procedure, promise an outcome, or transfer responsibility to any reader.
